# KindAI Market Reset Plan

**Decision date:** 26 September 2026  
**Owner:** Matthew Symons  
**Status:** Local release controls implemented and test suite passing; **not ready for public paid traffic or production checkout until staging, legal and operational gates pass**  
**Commercial target:** Five paid founding cabinet and joinery businesses

---

## 1. The Decision

> Relaunch KindAI through a founder-led **Cabinet & Joinery Founding Workflow Setup**, priced at **A$2,500 plus GST**. Sell five places directly. Do not restart with a cheap trial, a broad “AI for all tradies” campaign, or cold paid ads.

This is the shortest credible route to revenue and proof.

| Choice | Decision |
|---|---|
| Initial buyer | Australian cabinet-making or commercial-joinery owner who still controls estimating |
| Initial problem | Turning plans, price-book data and business rules into a reviewable first estimate draft |
| Initial offer | One configured workflow, one user, two reviewed real jobs and 30 days of email support |
| Included product access | Six months of Sole Tradie, then A$149/month only if the customer chooses to continue |
| Price | A$2,500 ex GST; A$2,750 charged |
| Capacity | Five paid businesses |
| First sales route | Warm introductions, targeted founder outreach and two trusted industry referrers |
| Proof | Paid use on two distinct jobs, corrections recorded, no invented performance claims |
| Free access | Public representative sample only; no private uploads and no live public AI |

**Why A$2,500, not A$1,000:** six months of Sole Tradie has a A$894 headline value before founder delivery. A$1,000 would leave A$106 for setup, two reviewed jobs and support. That is cheap positioning and poor delivery economics. A$2,500 remains a hypothesis, not a proven market-clearing price, but it is commercially testable without disguising free implementation.

## 2. What the Research Actually Supports

### Supported

- Buyers already pay for construction estimating software. Buildxact publicly lists Australian plans from A$199/month excluding GST, but it is a broader product and not a like-for-like KindAI comparison.
- Competitor demos from Togal and Kreo preserve human review and correction. This supports KindAI’s “reviewable draft” boundary, not an accuracy or time-saving claim.
- Australian construction AI adoption is still limited. This weakens generic AI advertising and strengthens founder-led proof.
- ACFA says its network spans sole traders, cabinetmakers, joiners, manufacturers, software/material suppliers, trainers and larger businesses. It runs events, training and industry connections.
- ACFA reported that AWISA members voted to merge into ACFA in January 2026, concentrating manufacturing and supplier relationships in one industry body.
- AWISA’s official description confirms a relevant supplier network across machinery, panels, hardware, software, components and safety equipment.
- Public sitemap analysis shows established competitors have much larger content surfaces: KindAI’s corrected launch sitemap has nine public URLs versus 142 for Groundplan and 678 for Kreo in the captured data. SEO is a support channel, not the route to the first five customers.

### Not supported

- A measured Australian market size for AI estimating.
- KindAI accuracy, hours saved, win-rate lift, margin improvement or replacement of an estimator.
- Acceptance of A$149, A$450, A$1,499 or A$2,500 by a defined segment.
- KindAI traffic, keyword or backlink strength from paid tools. Ahrefs access was insufficient and Similarweb authentication failed, so those sections remain partial.
- Public proof that the current production product can deliver the founding workflow safely. Local code corrections are not production evidence.

## 3. The Offer

### Cabinet & Joinery Founding Workflow Setup

**A$2,500 plus GST — A$2,750 total**

Included:

1. One cabinet-making or commercial-joinery estimating workflow.
2. One named user and one business.
3. One 90-minute setup session.
4. Import of up to 150 authorised price-book rows.
5. Configuration of up to 20 written business rules.
6. Two reviewed real jobs from the same workflow.
7. A corrections and assumptions record for both jobs.
8. Thirty days of email support.
9. Six months of Sole Tradie access, capped at 20 AI plan readings per month.
10. No automatic renewal. Continued access is A$149/month only after an explicit customer decision.

Not included:

- A final bid, certified quantity survey, compliance approval or accuracy guarantee.
- Unlimited founder work, a second trade, a second workflow, ERP migration or custom integration.
- More than one user, more than two reviewed jobs or more than 150 price-book rows.
- Public case-study rights. These require separate written permission.

### Positioning

**Headline:**

> Build one estimating workflow around your plans, prices and rules.

**Subhead:**

> KindAI prepares a reviewable first draft. Your estimator checks every quantity, rate, assumption and exclusion before anything leaves the business.

**Offer line:**

> Five cabinet and joinery businesses. A$2,500 plus GST. One workflow, two reviewed jobs and six months of Sole Tradie.

Never say:

- “Quote in 60 seconds.”
- “80% faster.”
- “Accurate estimates.”
- “Never miss an item.”
- “Replace an estimator.”
- “Free trial.”
- “Unlimited users.”

## 4. The First Sales Route

### Route order

1. **Warm introductions:** existing cabinet, joinery, construction, supplier, estimator and software contacts.
2. **Founder outreach:** a hand-built list of 20 cabinet or joinery businesses that visibly quote plan-based work.
3. **Two trusted referrers:** independent estimators, Planit/PYTHA trainers or other cabinet-workflow advisers.
4. **Paid customer proof:** two jobs, corrections and permissioned evidence.
5. **Industry channel:** approach ACFA for an educational session or member resource only after two or three paid customers produce credible evidence.
6. **Organic proof content:** founder video, redacted screen capture and short written case note.
7. **SEO and partnerships:** build the durable channel after the first cohort.

### Why not paid ads first

Cold ads would pay to discover three unknowns at once: segment, offer and product reliability. Direct selling lets Matthew see objections, observe the workflow and stop unsafe claims. Do not spend on Meta or Google until all of these are true:

- At least three full-price payments.
- At least three customers complete job two.
- No critical privacy or trust incident.
- Median founder time is five hours or less per customer.
- A permissioned demonstration shows the real product and review process.
- The application-to-payment funnel works in production.

### Prospect list

Build exactly 30 names:

| Group | Count | Selection rule |
|---|---:|---|
| Warm business contacts | 10 | Can personally introduce a cabinet/joinery owner or estimator |
| Cabinet/joinery businesses | 10 | Australian, plan-based work, owner or estimator identifiable |
| Workflow referrers | 10 | Independent estimator, cabinet software trainer, supplier adviser or industry consultant |

Do not scrape personal emails, buy a list or send a bulk sequence. Five personal messages per working day is enough.

## 7. Days 31–90

### If the first cohort passes

1. Finish all five places; maximum cohort revenue is **A$12,500 ex GST**.
2. Turn each permissioned result into one short case note: workflow, starting condition, corrections, usable outcome and what remains manual.
3. Ask the best two customers for one industry introduction each.
4. Approach two workflow referrers with the evidence, not a commission-first pitch.
5. Approach ACFA with a practical education topic: **“How to evaluate AI-assisted estimating without trusting the first draft.”**
6. Publish one founder proof video and two cut-down clips per customer outcome.
7. Reprice the next cohort from measured founder time. If delivery exceeds five hours, narrow scope or move toward A$3,500–A$5,000; do not hide founder labour.
8. Test the A$149/month continuation decision at month six. Do not infer retention from the included period.

### If the cohort fails

- Stop ads and association outreach.
- Do not add more trades.
- Do not build integrations to rescue demand.
- Compare rejection and correction evidence.
- Fix one dominant failure, then run a smaller second test.
