## Editing Rules

- Use real footage, the real KindAI interface and the real logo only.
- Caption every spoken line. Use Nunito bold for key overlays and keep text within the centre safe area.
- Keep the page background light; reserve dark treatment for the product screen.
- Use the KindAI gradient only as a short underline or emphasis, not as a full-screen effect.
- Use straight cuts, one or two subtle push-ins and a cursor highlight. Avoid glitch, hologram and robot effects.
- Do not display a plan, customer name, rate, address or result without written permission.
- Do not say “accurate,” “saves X hours,” “replaces an estimator,” or “quotes in 60 seconds” unless the exact shown evaluation measured and supports that statement.
- Music is optional. If used, choose a licensed low-key instrumental under the voice.
