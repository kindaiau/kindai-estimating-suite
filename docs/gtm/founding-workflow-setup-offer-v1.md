## 1. Ideal Customer

An Australian cabinet-making or commercial-joinery business with one decision-maker, one repeated estimating workflow, a near-term real job, authorised plans and rates, and the ability to review the estimate. The first cohort is not open to every trade.

## 2. Problem and Outcome

**Problem:** The owner or estimator spends repeated effort turning plans, schedules, rates and business rules into a first-pass estimate, but generic software does not start from their workflow.

**Outcome:** KindAI configures one controlled cabinet or joinery estimating workflow and produces two human-reviewed estimate drafts from the customer's authorised source material.

**Not promised:** A finished autonomous bid, a guaranteed accuracy rate, a fixed time saving, a compliance determination, a win-rate improvement, supplier ordering, live accounting sync or replacement of an estimator.

## 9. Safety and Data Boundaries

Use only authorised inputs and approved systems. Keep assumptions and unresolved items visible. Treat every output as a draft. Require human review before export or sending. Stop immediately after a critical data incident, cross-customer exposure, invented source evidence or repeated material underquoting risk.

## 10. Economics Guardrail

The A$2,500 ex-GST price supports a maximum of **five founder hours** plus direct software and payment costs while targeting at least a 50% contribution margin before acquisition cost. Track real founder time. If delivery exceeds five hours per customer, shrink scope or raise the next cohort price; do not hide the overrun by valuing founder time at zero.

## 11. Success and Stop Gates

| Gate | Pass rule | Stop or repair rule |
|---|---|---|
| Paid demand | At least 3 of 5 places paid at full price | Unpaid interest is not demand |
| First usable outcome | At least 4 of 5 customers judge the first draft useful after review | Two repeated critical defects pause recruitment |
| Repeat use | At least 3 of 5 complete a distinct second job | No repeat use means workflow value is unproven |
| Founder time | Median no more than 5 hours per customer | Shrink scope or raise price |
| Contribution margin | At least 50% before acquisition | Do not scale below threshold |
| Safety | Zero critical privacy or trust incidents | Immediate pause |
| Continuation signal | At least 3 customers ask to continue after the included period | Do not infer retention from setup payment |

## 12. Continuation Decision

At Day 30 choose one outcome: scale the same narrow offer, continue narrow while one gate is unresolved, repair and rerun, or stop. Do not add trades, tiers or paid ads until the paid-demand, usable-output, repeat-use, economics and safety gates pass.
