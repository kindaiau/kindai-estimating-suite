# KindAI’s initial Australian route to market

> **Superseded on 26 September 2026:** this research established the case for a narrow, founder-assisted launch but its free residential-builder proof session is no longer the offer. The current decision is a fixed **A$2,500 plus GST Cabinet & Joinery Founding Workflow Setup**. See `docs/gtm/kindai-market-reset-plan.md`.

## The decision

Launch **one assisted pre-quote estimating lane for owner-operated Australian residential renovation builders who quote from PDF plans**.

- **Initial commercial lane:** a **reviewable plan-to-scope-and-quantity starting point for residential renovation quotes**. KindAI’s job is the pre-quote step: turn one supplied plan set into an inspectable draft that the builder reviews against their own interpretation and cost base. It is not an autonomous quote, an end-to-end job-management replacement, or a generic “AI for tradies” offer.
- **Initial customer profile:** an **Australian owner-operator residential renovation builder/carpenter** with a small team, who is personally responsible for itemised quotes, currently works from PDF plans with manual takeoff and/or spreadsheets, and can bring a live or recent plan-based job to a session. Prospects who do not quote from plans, cannot supply a representative plan, or only need simple field-service quotes are outside the first lane.
- **Initial proof mechanism:** a **scheduled, single-plan Estimating Proof Session**. After qualification, KindAI grants only the minimum supervised AI use needed to create one reviewable draft on the prospect’s own plan. The session must expose quantities, scope assumptions, exclusions and any uncertainty for the prospect to check. It must not provide a self-serve A$9 trial, an ongoing free workspace, or unrestricted AI usage.
- **Primary acquisition motion:** **founder-led referrals from independent construction estimators who already advise residential renovation builders**. Recruit a small number of estimators with relevant builder clients, equip them to qualify the profile above, and jointly run the real-plan proof. The estimator is a trusted introducer and review participant; the builder remains the prospective KindAI customer.

**Commercial offer after proof:** test only the existing **A$149/month Sole Tradie** tier, subject to the product actually being able to deliver the demonstrated workflow and support it. Treat its one-user/20-takeoff configuration as a price-and-capacity hypothesis, not an established market fit. Do not discount it through a substitute trial and do not lead with the A$450 Pro or A$1,499 Mid-Tier Builder tiers.

## Why this is the most credible initial route

### Verified facts

There is a paid Australian estimating category, but not a measured Australian AI-estimating market. Buildxact publicly lists Foundation, Pro and Master at A$199, A$399 and A$599 per month excluding GST, respectively, with a 12-month annual commitment and a broader workflow that includes takeoff, estimating and downstream job-management functions. This verifies that relevant buyers encounter paid estimating software; it does **not** validate KindAI’s price, quota, accuracy, customer outcomes or feature equivalence. [1]

The direct local pain signal is specific to quote production and scope clarity. In a June 2026 Australian small-carpentry discussion, the owner described smaller enquiries as capable of consuming “one or two full days” a week in visits and detailed quoting. The same discussion shows a buyer/tradie expectation for an itemised labour-and-material breakdown rather than an unexplained automated price. This is qualitative, non-representative evidence, but it fits the selected renovation-builder profile and the reviewable-output proof. [2]

The category’s credible competitors preserve human review. Togal’s own demo says it does not replace the estimator and leaves valuation and bid amounts with the user. Kreo’s demo likewise requires scale setting, selection/confidence tuning and manual correction, and warns that its automated measurement can be messy or incorrect on structural plans. These are competitor facts, not claims about KindAI, but they support a proof mechanism that makes review and exceptions visible rather than promising autonomous accuracy. [3] [4]

Acquisition evidence favours trust-based introductions over a broad demand-generation launch. Buildxact publicly groups accounting firms, coaches/consultants, industry groups, construction estimators and education/training organisations in its partner programme, with a referral-link mechanism. ServiceM8’s Australian programme further shows that partner enablement, training and implementation work are part of the category’s channel model. Neither programme proves that a KindAI partner will refer or convert buyers; they establish that a construction-software adviser/referral route is real and operationally credible. [5] [6]

Broad paid acquisition lacks an evidence base. In the Australia, 12-month Google Trends comparison captured in the research, “estimating software Australia” and “quoting software tradies” were reported at zero relative interest, while “AI estimating” had low-volume interest; Google warns that Trends is sampled and normalised, not search volume. Construction businesses also reported 6% AI use in 2024–25, versus 12% across all businesses. These facts do not mean there is no demand. They do mean generic AI language and broad search spend are not validated as the launch engine. [7] [8]

### Route logic — inference to test, not a proven result

The selected route combines the most directly evidenced problem with the smallest credible commercial commitment. The target buyer has a real plan, owns the quote-risk decision and can inspect whether the proposed draft is useful before being asked to subscribe. This avoids asking a mainstream construction buyer—where AI use is still limited—to trust a generic AI claim. It also avoids trying to out-feature Buildxact’s end-to-end platform or Groundplan’s established takeoff workflow on day one. Groundplan’s displayed A$99/month marketplace anchor (last updated July 2025) and seven-day trial reinforce that KindAI’s A$149 offer cannot win merely by being another plan-measurement utility; it needs a constrained, defensible proof around the buyer’s actual quote. [9]

An independent construction estimator is the single best first introducer for this lane because the adviser is close to takeoff, scope and estimate review. The recommendation does **not** assume the adviser wants to refer a tool that may affect their work. It should be tested as a complementary workflow: the adviser helps the builder assess an inspectable starting point, rather than being asked to endorse an unreviewable automated estimate or surrender professional judgement.

The A$149 tier is the only prudent initial commercial test. It sits below Buildxact’s published A$199 Foundation headline but has a different seat count, AI allowance, billing structure and feature surface, so it is not a like-for-like value claim. The price should be presented plainly, after the real-plan review, with no “cheaper than Buildxact” message. The test question is narrow: will this profile pay A$149 per month for the demonstrated, bounded workflow? No evidence supports assuming the answer is yes.

## The single-plan Estimating Proof Session

The proof is a sales-and-learning mechanism, not a product-performance promise.

1. **Qualify before access.** Confirm that the prospect is the residential renovation builder described above; has a real PDF plan; currently produces itemised quotes; and will personally review the output. Decline simple service-quote, civil, enterprise and no-plan opportunities for this first lane.
2. **Use one representative plan set.** The founder or trained referring estimator runs the session. Free AI use is limited to the actions required for that one proof; there is no reusable free allowance or cheap trial subscription.
3. **Make the draft inspectable.** Review the plan-derived scope/quantities, assumptions, exclusions and uncertain items with the buyer. The buyer compares it with their own interpretation, cost base and quote process. Do not present the output as a final bid, a guarantee of completeness, or a substitute for estimator judgement.
4. **Ask for the paid decision.** If the buyer judges the supported workflow usable, present the A$149/month Sole Tradie offer with its current one-user and 20-takeoff limits. If the product cannot truthfully support the shown workflow, do not sell the subscription.
5. **Record the evidence.** For every qualified referral, record qualification outcome, plan received, session completed, buyer-reported usefulness, paid decision, rejection reason and second-month renewal. This is the practical evidence chain needed to replace an assumed TAM.

A completed proof does not establish accuracy, time saved, margin improvement or retention. Those outcomes remain unknown until they are measured on completed customer work. A positive first session also does not authorise reuse of the builder’s plan or data beyond the consented evaluation and product terms.

## What must be deferred

- **All other customer segments.** Do not launch to “all tradies,” field-service businesses, commercial/civil estimators, multi-user builders, or every plan-based trade. The first route is only owner-operated residential renovation builders/carpenters who personally quote from plans.
- **Pro, Mid-Tier Builder and Enterprise sales.** There is no evidence that the A$450 or A$1,499 configurations, seat counts or takeoff allowances are accepted. The A$1,499 tier is especially difficult to defend against Buildxact’s publicly listed A$599 Master plan without verified workflow, service or data value. [1]
- **Paid ads and broad search acquisition.** Do not run paid ads before the proof produces paid conversions and renewal evidence. Do not use generic “AI estimating” or “tradie quoting software” search demand as a budget or pipeline forecast. [7]
- **National association sponsorship or media.** HIA and Master Builders provide real access surfaces, but their audience figures do not establish qualified buyer reach, commercial terms, lead quality or conversion for KindAI. A national sponsorship should not become the launch dependency. [11] [12]
- **Xero App Store, supplier/merchant and marketplace routes.** These are later milestones, not initial acquisition motions. Xero listing requires a genuine, supportable integration and partner process; supplier distribution needs a concrete pricing or estimate-collaboration benefit, not a generic AI endorsement. [13] [14]
- **End-to-end job-management or integration claims.** Do not imply scheduling, purchasing, invoicing, accounting sync, supplier pricing, local rate libraries or a system-of-record replacement unless those capabilities are already live, reliable and supportable.
- **Autonomy and outcome claims.** Do not promise accuracy, saved hours, fewer omissions, protected margin, win-rate lift or AI-generated final bids. The proof must show reviewability and boundaries, not manufacture a performance claim.

## Unknowns that the first route must resolve

- Whether the selected profile will pay **A$149/month** after a real-plan proof, accept the 20-takeoff cap, and renew after a second month.
- Whether KindAI can reliably handle the relevant Australian residential renovation plan quality, symbols, revisions, scope ambiguity and difficult exceptions—and how much human correction is required.
- Whether the product can securely support the proposed supervised evaluation, plan/data handling, onboarding and response expectations. This report makes no assumption that these controls already exist.
- Whether a reviewable draft fits the builder’s existing spreadsheet, pricing and quote workflow without creating duplicate entry. Any export or integration must be demonstrated before it is used in the sales claim.
- Whether independent construction estimators view the workflow as complementary, will make referrals, and can do so with commercially workable enablement and support ownership.
- Whether early paid users are representative enough to expand beyond this profile. Construction-sector size, review-site ratings, competitor prices and partner pages do not answer that question.

**Decision rule:** keep the route narrow until repeated adviser-referred, real-plan proofs produce paid A$149 activations and second-month renewals without unsupported service or product promises. If they do not, diagnose the observed failure—profile fit, proof credibility, product capability, price, quota or partner incentive—before adding a segment, channel, feature claim or acquisition spend.
