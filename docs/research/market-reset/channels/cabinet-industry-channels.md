# Australian Cabinet and Joinery Channel Evidence

**Captured:** 26 September 2026

## Australian Cabinet and Furniture Association (ACFA)

**Official source:** https://www.acfa.net.au/about/

ACFA states that it has supported the furniture, cabinet-making, kitchen, joinery and design industries since 1948. Its membership ranges from sole traders and small family businesses through to larger manufacturers, suppliers and consultants. Services include workplace support, training, technical information, professional development, national and local events, awards and connections between designers, manufacturers and suppliers. Its listed member categories include cabinetmakers, joiners, furniture manufacturers, designers, installers, machinery/material/hardware/software suppliers and industry services. Listed industry partners include Planit, PYTHA, Lincoln Sentry and other industry service providers.

**Commercial implication:** ACFA is a high-fit credibility and referral channel after KindAI has paid proof. It is not the first place to ask for broad promotion without a permissioned case study.

## ACFA and AWISA Merger

**Official source:** https://www.acfa.net.au/awisa-members-vote-to-merge-with-acfa/

ACFA reported on 4 March 2026 that AWISA members formally voted in favour of a merger at an extraordinary general meeting on 15 January 2026. The announcement says the merged organisation connects manufacturers, cabinetmakers, designers and suppliers across Australia and that integration work would continue over the following months.

**Commercial implication:** The merged network concentrates both manufacturing buyers and technology/material suppliers in one industry body. A practical education session based on measured estimating workflow evidence is more credible than a generic AI sponsorship pitch.

## Australian Woodworking Industry Suppliers Association (AWISA)

**Official source:** https://www.awisa.com/association.html

AWISA states that it was formed in 1986 by principal suppliers to the Australian woodworking industry. Its purposes include exhibitions, industry magazines, cooperation with related organisations and ethical standards. Member categories include woodworking machinery, cabinet and furniture hardware, panel products, surface materials, tools, computer software, materials handling, dust extraction, components and safety equipment.

**Commercial implication:** Supplier and software relationships can provide trusted introductions to cabinet businesses. Start with direct introductions through existing suppliers or software trainers; pursue publication or association activity after the first case evidence exists.
