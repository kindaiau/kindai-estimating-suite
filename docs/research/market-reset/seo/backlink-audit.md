**Report date:** 26 September 2026

**Competitor sections approved:** Yes, Buildxact and Groundplan  
**Status:** Blocked by source access, no backlink metrics verified

## 1. Executive Summary

The KindAI backlink profile cannot be rated from the available evidence. Ahrefs accepted the connector but returned an insufficient-plan response for the required domain rating, backlink statistics, referring-domain history and competitor pulls. No totals, authority score, three-month gains or losses, anchors, risk bands or linked-page data were available, so none are estimated here.

No active backlink strategy can be verified. Public sitemaps confirm that KindAI, Buildxact, Groundplan and Kreo publish crawlable pages, but a sitemap is not backlink evidence. Competitor benchmarking and link-gap work are therefore blocked, not negative findings. This audit should be rerun from an Ahrefs plan with Site Explorer API access or from exported referring-domain, backlink, anchor and best-by-links files.
