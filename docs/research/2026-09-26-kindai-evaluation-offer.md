# KindAI Evaluation Offer Research

**Research date:** 26 September 2026  
**Author:** Manus AI

## Best fit for KindAI

Replace the A$9 trial and open-ended free AI access with one offer:

> **Book a Live Plan Evaluation**  
> Bring one real job. A KindAI specialist will run one representative plan package with you, explain the assumptions, and show how the estimate is assembled. Approved businesses then receive a seven-day private evaluation workspace with one named user, one project, and three AI estimating runs. Export, integrations, collaboration, and further runs unlock on a paid plan.

This is the strongest fit because KindAI is selling a high-value estimating system, not a low-cost utility. A serious buyer needs proof on a relevant plan. KindAI needs to control AI cost, support effort, data risk, and people using the evaluation to complete unpaid tender work.

## What the market shows

There are three common entry models.

**Short, broad free trials** are used by Buildxact and Tradify. Both advertise 14 days with no credit card, while Groundplan and Kreo use seven-day trials.[1] [2] [3] [4] This reduces signup friction, but the unrestricted versions are a poor fit for KindAI because every real plan run has a direct AI cost and can produce commercially useful work.

**Usage-limited evaluation** is the stronger control pattern. STACK limits free access to seven days, two concurrent projects, and ten takeoffs per project. It also withholds higher-value AI functionality from free access.[5] This shows that a construction-software buyer can evaluate a workflow without receiving the complete paid product.

**Guided proof on the buyer's own data** is the most premium pattern. Togal.AI leads with a booked demo using the prospect's own drawings rather than a public free trial.[6] Fergus combines a 14-day trial with specialist onboarding and a sample playground.[7] The useful lesson is not to copy an unlimited trial. It is to combine relevant proof, guidance, and clear limits.

## Recommended public offer

Use this public copy:

> **Prove KindAI on one real job.**  
> Book a 30-minute Live Plan Evaluation with a KindAI specialist. We will test one representative plan package, show the assumptions behind the draft, and map the right plan for your estimating volume. Approved Australian businesses receive seven days to review the result and run up to three controlled refinements. No credit card. No open-ended free account.

Primary CTA: **Book a Live Plan Evaluation**  
Secondary CTA: **Explore an Interactive Sample**

The interactive sample should use pre-built scenarios only. It can let visitors change trade, labour rate, markup, and pricing view, but it should not accept private plans or call a paid AI model.

## Guardrails

- Require business name, ABN, work email, mobile, trade, team size, monthly quote volume, current workflow, and purchase timing.
- Require confirmation that the applicant is authorised to use the submitted plans.
- Approve one evaluation per business within 12 months.
- Limit the private workspace to one user, one project, 25 pages, and three server-enforced AI runs.
- Keep outputs in-app and read-only during evaluation. Disable PDF export, accounting integrations, API access, collaboration, and bulk data import.
- Activate the seven-day workspace only after the guided session is attended.
- Do not offer a discount, free month, or automatically renewing trial.
- Position it as an evaluation of paid software, not a free plan.

## Why this should convert better

The offer reduces buyer risk without reducing KindAI's perceived value. The prospect sees the product working on a relevant job, receives expert help at the moment of activation, and invests effort in configuring a real workflow. That commitment is more meaningful than entering a card for a cheap trial.

It also creates a clean sales conversation. The results review can lead directly to Sole Tradie at A$149, Pro at A$450, Mid-Tier Builder at A$1,499, or a custom enterprise scope. No awkward jump is required from a heavily discounted product to premium pricing.

## First metrics to track

- Completed applications and approval rate.
- Approved evaluations that book and attend the session.
- Workspaces completing a first plan run within 48 hours.
- Evaluations using at least two of the three allowed runs.
- Results-review attendance.
- Paid conversion within 7 and 30 days.
- AI cost and specialist time per activated evaluation and per paid conversion.
- Duplicate applications, limit attempts, and export attempts.

Use the first 30–50 completed evaluations to decide whether three runs and seven days are correct. Do not increase the allowance because prospects ask for more; increase it only if verified buyers consistently fail to reach proof of value within the current limit.
