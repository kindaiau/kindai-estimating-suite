# KindAI pricing

Last updated: 26 September 2026

All prices are in Australian dollars. Subscription prices exclude GST. Product outputs are estimate drafts for human review. KindAI does not provide final autonomous bids.

## Interactive sample

- Price: No charge
- Access: Representative estimating sample with adjustable labour, markup and pricing assumptions
- Limits: No private-plan upload, project storage, export or open-ended free AI access
- Explore: https://kindaiestimator.com/demo

## Cabinet and Joinery Founding Workflow Setup

- Status: Application and administrator approval required
- Price excluding GST: A$2,500 one time
- GST: A$250
- Total payment: A$2,750
- Included: One cabinet or commercial-joinery estimating workflow, one user, up to 150 price-book rows, up to 20 business rules, one founder-led setup session, two reviewed real jobs, 30 days of email support and the first six months of Sole Tradie
- Input limit: Up to five supported PDF, JPG or PNG files, maximum 32MB per file
- Renewal: No automatic renewal at the end of the included period
- Apply: https://kindaiestimator.com/evaluation

Checkout is not public. An administrator may issue a payment invitation only after the application and fixed scope are approved.

## Sole Tradie

- Price: A$149 per month, or A$1,430.40 per year billed annually
- Included users: 1
- AI Vision plan readings: 20 per month
- Projects: Unlimited
- Includes: Quick Quote, company price book and instructions, correction history, GST calculations, PDF quote export and labour-rate workflow

## Important limits

- Maximum supported upload size: 32MB per file
- AI output: Draft only; the customer remains responsible for reviewing quantities, scope, exclusions, rates, compliance and the final quote
- Availability: Features and integrations must be confirmed before they are relied on
